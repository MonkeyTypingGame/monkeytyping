## What is MonkeyTyping?

[MonkeyTyping](https://monkeytyping.net/) is a free online tool to **measure your typing speed, practise with real passages in 50 languages, and build speed and accuracy with games, lessons and listening-based drills.**

Choose a 15, 30 or 60 second test, type a set number of words, or practise with quotes and long scripts. Every result is calculated in your browser, so a run starts the moment you press a key and nothing has to be downloaded.

**Highlights**

- ⚡ **Live stats**: WPM, raw WPM, accuracy and consistency update as you type
- 🎯 **Four content sources**: Time, Words, Quote and Script modes, plus Numbers and Punctuation toggles
- 🌍 **50 languages**, including English, German, Spanish, Chinese, Japanese, Urdu, Hindi and Arabic
- 🎮 **Keyboard Warrior** (1,000 levels with multiplayer) and **7 solo typing games** with 500 levels each
- 🎧 **Listening-based practice**: 10,000 sentences and 6,597 vocabulary words
- 📘 **50 typing lessons** from the home row to digits and symbols
- 🏆 **Leaderboard** with All Time, Weekly and Daily views
- 🆓 **Free to use**, no account needed to test or practise

## How Scores Are Calculated

MonkeyTyping counts **one word as five characters**, including spaces and punctuation. This keeps scores comparable no matter how long or short the actual words are.

### WPM (your main score)

WPM counts **only correctly typed characters**:

```
WPM = (correct characters ÷ 5) ÷ minutes
```

**Example:** In a 60-second test you type 250 characters correctly.

```
(250 ÷ 5) ÷ 1 minute = 50 WPM
```

In a 30-second test, the same 250 correct characters would give `(250 ÷ 5) ÷ 0.5 = 100 WPM`.

### Raw WPM

Raw WPM counts **every character you typed**, including mistakes:

```
Raw WPM = (all typed characters ÷ 5) ÷ minutes
```

The gap between Raw WPM and WPM shows how much speed you lose to mistakes. A small gap means you are typing cleanly.

### Accuracy

Accuracy shows how clean your typing was. A fast run with many mistakes still leaves you slower in real use, because you have to go back and fix errors.

### Consistency

Consistency shows how **steady your speed was** across the test. A high score means you kept an even pace instead of bursting and stalling.

### Summary

| Metric | What it shows | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| **WPM** | Correct characters only | Your real, usable typing speed |
| **Raw WPM** | All typed characters | Your hand speed before errors |
| **Accuracy** | How clean your typing was | Tells you if a fast run was also a good one |
| **Consistency** | Variation of speed across the test | How steady your rhythm is |

> 💡 **Tip:** Accuracy matters as much as speed. Check it next to your WPM after every run.

## What Is a Good Typing Speed?

| WPM | Level |
|---|---|
| 0 – 25 | 🐢 Beginner |
| 25 – 40 | 🙂 Average |
| 40 – 60 | 👍 Above average |
| 60 – 80 | 🚀 Fast |
| 80 – 100 | 🔥 Very fast |
| 100+ | 🏆 Elite |

The average typist types around **40 WPM**; **60 WPM** and above is considered fast.

> Typing numbers and symbols is where accuracy usually drops first, so expect a lower WPM in Numbers and Punctuation runs.

## Test Modes

Every option sits in the same config bar on the [main test](https://monkeytyping.net/).

| Mode | Options | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| **Time** | 15 · 30 · 60 seconds | Quick benchmarks. The 60 second option works as a standard 1 minute typing test |
| **Words** | 12 · 25 · 50 · 100 · Custom (1 to 1000) | A steady warm-up with a clear finish line |
| **Quote** | Short · Medium · Large | Practising real passages |
| **Script** | Easy · Medium · Hard (browse and pick) | Repeating the same longer passage and comparing attempts |

**Extra toggles** (Time and Words mode only):

| Toggle | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Punctuation** | Adds symbols to the text |
| **Numbers** | Swaps the text for whole numbers, decimals, hex `0x`, binary `0b`, base32 codes and `H:MM` times |

## Practice Tools

The [practice area](https://monkeytyping.net/practice) is where the structured work happens. Both systems use text-to-speech, so you type from what you **hear** rather than from what you can already read.

### Sentence Typing Practice

| Feature | Details |
|---|---|
| **Content** | 10,000 sentences across 25 parent categories and 238 child categories |
| **Sets** | Up to 6 sets per child category, 10 sentences per set |
| **How it works** | Listen to each sentence, then type what you heard. No countdown |
| **Scoring** | 10 points per correct sentence. Skipping is allowed but counts as an error |

[**Practice Typing Sentences →**](https://monkeytyping.net/sentences)

### Vocabulary Typing Practice

| Feature | Details |
|---|---|
| **Content** | 6,597 words across 109 parent and child categories |
| **Sets** | Up to 17 sets per category, 10 words per set |
| **Word phase** | The word is spoken once. Type the word you hear |
| **Sentence phase** | A sentence using the word repeats until you have typed it, against a 30 second countdown |
| **Out of time** | The sentence is revealed for 7 seconds, then the set moves on |

[**Practice Vocabulary Typing →**](https://monkeytyping.net/vocabulary)

## Leaderboard

The [leaderboard](https://monkeytyping.net/leaderboard) ranks your **best run per player**, so the board reflects a real best rather than a lucky single attempt.

| View | What it shows |
|---|---|

## Tips to Type Faster

1. **Check accuracy next to WPM.** A fast but messy run is slower in real use.
2. **Learn touch typing.** Keep your fingers on the home row and work through the [Lessons](https://monkeytyping.net/lessons).
3. **Practise a little every day.** Short, regular sessions beat one long session.
4. **Repeat the same mode.** Small improvements are easy to spot when the rules do not change.
5. **Train your weak spots.** Use Numbers and Punctuation toggles for symbols, and [Sentence](https://monkeytyping.net/sentences) or [Vocabulary](https://monkeytyping.net/vocabulary) practice for listening and recall.
6. **Keep a steady rhythm.** Watch your Consistency score, because even pacing beats bursts.
